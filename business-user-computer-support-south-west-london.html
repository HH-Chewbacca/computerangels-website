IT support for small businesses

We look after small London businesses under a Guardian Angel service level agreement: one monthly fee, a guaranteed response, and an IT department on the end of the phone.

Guardian Angel support

The products and problems in a small business are different from those in a large one, and we don't believe you can be an expert in both. That's why we specialise in small businesses, on both PC and Mac.

A Guardian Angel agreement makes us your IT department. For a fixed monthly fee you get:

  • A four-hour response guarantee when something goes wrong
  • Free telephone and remote support for break-fix issues
  • Free Microsoft 365 support
  • 25% off call-out charges when an engineer needs to visit
  • One itemised invoice a month, so you always know what you are paying for

Business agreements run for a minimum of 12 months, then roll on from month to month.

Why we price per user

Our support is built around people, not machines. It's the person we're helping, as well as the computer, so you pay for each person we look after and that covers the computers and mobile devices they use.

Guardian Angel for business: your questions answered

What does the four-hour response guarantee mean?

When you report a critical issue, meaning one that stops your business from working, we will begin working on it within four working hours. Working hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm, excluding bank holidays. It is a guarantee of how quickly we respond, not of how long the fix will take.

What counts as a user?

One person and the computers and mobile devices they use. You pay for each person we look after, not for each machine.

What counts as a break-fix issue?

Something that was working and has stopped. Telephone and remote support for break-fix issues is included in your monthly fee.

What is charged on top?

On-site visits, new equipment and user setups, migrations, projects and training are charged at £75 an hour plus VAT, which is 25% off our standard rate. Parts, software and licences are charged at cost as quoted.

What happens when someone joins or leaves?

Tell us and we adjust your subscription. The monthly fee follows the number of people and servers we look after.

Is there a minimum term?

Yes. Business agreements run for a minimum of 12 months. After that the agreement rolls on from month to month and you can cancel at any time, with cover continuing to the end of the month you have paid for.

Is Microsoft 365 support included?

Yes. Support for Microsoft 365 email and Office apps is included for every user.

Do you cover people working from home?

Yes. Cover follows the person, so their computers and mobile devices are included wherever they work.

How do we get started?

Use the calculator to get an estimate, then ask for a quote. We will confirm the price in writing and set up a monthly Direct Debit.
