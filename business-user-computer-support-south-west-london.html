IT support for small businesses

We look after small London businesses under a Guardian Angel service level agreement: one monthly fee, a guaranteed response, and an IT department on the end of the phone.

Guardian Angel support

The products and problems in a small business are different from those in a large one, and we don't believe you can be an expert in both. That's why we specialise in small businesses, on both PC and Mac.

A Guardian Angel agreement makes us your IT department. For a fixed monthly fee you get:

  • A four-hour response guarantee when something goes wrong
  • Free telephone and remote support for break-fix issues
  • Free Microsoft 365 support
  • 25% off call-out charges when an engineer needs to visit
  • One itemised invoice a month, so you always know what you are paying for

Why we price per user

Our support is built around people, not machines. It's the person we're helping, as well as the computer, so you pay for each person we look after and that covers the computers and mobile devices they use.
