IT support for small businesses

On-site and remote support for small London businesses, from people who specialise in exactly that.

What we do

The products and problems in a small business are different from those in a large one, and we don't believe you can be an expert in both. That's why we specialise in small businesses and home users, on both PC and Mac.

We look after your computers, network, email, backup and security, and we're there when something breaks. Every visit is noted and every invoice is itemised, so you always know what you are paying for.

Guardian Angel support contracts

A Guardian Angel contract makes us your IT department for a fixed monthly fee.

  • Your calls go into our priority queue
  • Phone and remote support are included
  • Chargeable work is billed at a reduced hourly rate
  • One itemised invoice each month

The monthly fee depends on the size of your business. Ask us for a quote.
