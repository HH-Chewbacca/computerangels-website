Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and who we share it with.

Last updated November 2026.

This policy explains how we handle personal information under UK data protection law: the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Computer Angels is the trading name of Computer Angels Limited (company number 5323507), 160 Munster Road, London SW6 5RA. We are the data controller for the information described here and are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). You can contact us on 020 7736 0800 or through our contact form.

Information you give us through this website

If you use the contact form we collect your name, telephone number, email address, your company name if you give one, and your message. We use this only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to book and carry out the work. The form is delivered to our office mailbox by Web3Forms, which processes the message on our behalf.

Information we hold about customers

When you become a customer we keep a record in our internal customer system. This includes your contact details, the devices we have worked on and what we did, appointments, emails between us about your work, and your invoices and payments. Where we need them to do the work, we also record passwords for equipment or services we look after; these are stored securely.

After we work with you we may send you an invitation to review us on Trustpilot. You can ask us not to.

We keep invoices and payment records for seven years because the law requires it. We keep other records for as long as you are a customer and for a reasonable period afterwards. Backups of your data taken in our workshop are normally kept for around three months.

Our lawful basis under UK GDPR

We use your information to answer your enquiry and carry out work you have asked for (contract), to keep the accounting records the law requires (legal obligation), and to run and improve our service, including asking for reviews (legitimate interests).

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with suppliers who help us provide our service, and only as far as they need it:

  • Web3Forms, to deliver contact form messages
  • GoCardless, if you set up a Direct Debit for a Guardian Angel subscription
  • Trustpilot, to send review invitations
  • Suppliers of services we set up for you, such as Microsoft, Livedrive and ESET
  • Our accountants and, where required, HM Revenue & Customs

Information held outside the UK

Some of the suppliers listed above store or process information outside the UK, including in the United States and the European Union. Where that happens we rely on the safeguards UK data protection law provides, such as UK adequacy decisions and approved contract terms.

Cookies and other sites

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Some pages include content from other companies, which may set their own cookies or record that you visited: the Trustpilot review widget, the Google map on our contact page, and the links to GoCardless and AnyDesk. Their own privacy policies apply to what they collect.

The website is hosted by GitHub, which keeps technical logs of visits, including IP addresses, for security.

Your rights under UK GDPR

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where we do not need to keep it by law, and object to us using it. To do any of these, call us or use the contact form.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
