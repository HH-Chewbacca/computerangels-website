COMPUTERANGELS

Episode IV

A New Page

It is a period of great uncertainty on the website.

People apparently typing random addresses are finding themselves exploring new worlds, new life and new civilizations, and boldly going where no man has gone before.

Yes, I know. That's from the wrong canon, but we're hardly likely to miss the opportunity to rub the fanboys up the wrong way.

Live long and prosper.

While we have your attention, and since this page was never supposed to exist, allow us to explain what has happened.

You asked for a page. Our server, a loyal but literal-minded droid, searched every corner of the website. It looked behind the contact form. It checked under the price list. It found nothing, panicked quietly, and sent you here instead.

This is not the page you are looking for.

It may have moved. It may have been renamed. It may never have existed at all, in which case, well done: you have discovered a region of cyberspace that even our own engineers have never visited.

Do not be alarmed. Getting lost is what we deal with for a living. Every day, people arrive at our shop in Fulham with computers that have wandered far from where they should be. Email that has vanished. Printers that have turned to the dark side. Wi-Fi that works in every room except the one you are sitting in.

We bring them back. We have been doing it since 2004, mostly without the use of a lightsaber.

Before you ask: yes, we have tried turning it off and on again. It is the first thing they teach us.

So take a moment. Enjoy the stars. Somewhere out there is the page you actually wanted, and the fastest way to find it is the menu at the top of this one.

If that fails, call us on 020 7736 0800 and a real human being will answer. No droids.

Until then, may your backups be recent, your passwords be long, and your broadband be with you. Always.

The End

(It will now start again. Space is like that.)
