What we do

When things start to go wrong with your computer it's difficult to know who to trust. Since 2004 Computer Angels has looked after home users in Fulham, Putney, Battersea, Hammersmith, Kensington, Chelsea and across London.

Your engineer will explain the problem in language you can understand. We remember that it's the customer we're helping, as well as the computer, and no job is too small.

Call us and we'll take a few details. Often we can fix the problem over the phone or by remote support. If not, we'll book a convenient time for one of our Angels to visit.

Computer Angels was founded on the principle that home and small business users shouldn't have to be experts to safely use their computers.

Guardian Angel: your questions answered

What is Guardian Angel?

It is a monthly subscription that works like breakdown cover for your computer. You pay a small amount each month, and when something goes wrong you call us and we sort it out by phone or remote support at no extra charge.

What counts as a break-fix problem?

Something that was working and has stopped. For example: you cannot get into your email, the printer has stopped printing, a program will not open, or the computer has slowed to a crawl.

What is not included?

Setting up new computers, phones, software or accounts, moving you to a new system, training, and the cost of any parts, software or licences. Members get 25% off our standard rates for all of that.

What happens if you need to visit me?

Call-outs are not included in the monthly fee, but members get 25% off. That makes our hourly rate £90 including VAT instead of £120.

Does it cover all my devices?

Yes. One subscription covers one person and the computers and mobile devices they use. If someone else in your household wants cover, they need their own subscription.

When can I call?

During our opening hours: Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm, and Saturday 9.30am to 5.30pm.

How do I pay?

£36 a month including VAT, by Direct Debit. We use GoCardless, and you can set it up online in a couple of minutes.

Is there a minimum term? How do I cancel?

There is no minimum term. The subscription runs from month to month and you can cancel at any time by telling us. Your cover continues to the end of the month you have paid for.

Is Microsoft 365 support really free?

Yes. Help with your Microsoft 365 email and Office apps is included for members.

No fix, no fee

If we can't fix your problem, or can't diagnose it in the first thirty minutes, we won't charge you for that time. Some conditions apply. See the guarantee
